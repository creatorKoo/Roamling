# Bori artwork

Bori (보리, called Mochi in file names and earlier releases) is Roamling's
built-in mascot. The character and every drawing of it are
**Copyright (C) 2026 GooBeom Jeoung. All rights reserved.**

The GNU GPL in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers Roamling's source code. It does not
cover this artwork. This is the separate notice that
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) says official mascot assets may carry, and it
controls for the files identified below.

## What this covers

- the Bori character design itself;
- the built-in sprite sheets, pose sheets and atlases in
  `Sources/RoamlingPet/Resources/BuiltInPets/`;
- the source strips, candidate frames and cuts under `assets/mochi/`;
- any copy of those images embedded in a Roamling binary, app bundle, APK or
  pet package, in any size or recolouring; and
- future Bori artwork added to this repository, unless a file says otherwise.

It does not cover the paw mark (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)), the code
that loads and draws pets, or pet packages made by other people, which stay
under their own authors' licenses.

## What you may do

- Run official Roamling builds, with Bori in them.
- Build Roamling from this repository and run it yourself, with Bori in it, for
  personal use and for preparing contributions.
- Show Bori in screenshots, recordings, reviews and articles about Roamling.

## What needs written permission

- Distributing the artwork, or a modified version of it, in a fork, another
  application, a pet package, or on its own.
- Any commercial use: selling it, selling something that contains it, or using
  it to promote something else.
- Merchandise, stickers, emoji sets, or a new character derived from Bori.

A fork that is distributed to others should replace the built-in pet with
artwork its maintainers have the right to ship. The pet format is documented
in [docs/pets.md](docs/pets.md), and Roamling also loads packages from disk.
